What Dock can do

Every feature — and where to find it. Inside Dock the same window opens with a right-click on “Programs” → “What Dock can do”.

Appearance
Looks in one click

Wallpaper, color, dock, icons, the Island and widgets change at once. There are 8 designer looks: Cyberpunk, Pastel, Glass, Retro game…

📍 Where: Tray → “Looks…”
“Surprise me”

A random but coherent look: color, dock and icon shape, the Island, wallpaper. Don't like it — press again.

📍 Where: Settings → Appearance → “Your style”
Edit desktop

Move widgets, the dock and the Island with the regular mouse right on the screen, add stickers and labels.

📍 Where: Right-click “Programs” in the dock → “Edit desktop”
Dock shape

Plate, capsule, tiles, neon, “floating” icons without a plate or a full-width bar.

📍 Where: Settings → Appearance → “Dock shape”
Dock at the bottom, top, left or right

Hold Alt and drag the dock to any screen edge. Or choose the edge in settings.

📍 Where: Alt + mouse, or Settings → Appearance → “Screen edge”
Icon under the mouse

Grows like on a Mac, lifts up or glows in the theme color. You can also change how much it grows.

📍 Where: Settings → Appearance → “Icon under the mouse”
Icon packs

All icons in one style: neon, pastel, glass, pixels, light, single-color, tile, gray.

📍 Where: Settings → Appearance → “Icon style”
Icon shape

Circle, square or rounded square — different icons line up in one even row.

📍 Where: Settings → Appearance → “Icon shape”
Custom icon for a program

Any picture, emoji or a couple of letters instead of the icon — for example 🎮 for games.

📍 Where: Right-click an icon in the dock → “Custom icon…” or “Icon from emoji or letters…”
Dock plate color

Any color just for the dock, regardless of the theme. And density — from glass to solid.

📍 Where: Settings → Appearance → “Dock plate”
Your own font

Any font from your computer — for the Island, widgets, menus and settings.

📍 Where: Settings → Appearance → “Font”
Interface sounds

Quiet clicks on hover and launch: soft, mechanical, 8-bit — or your own WAV files.

📍 Where: Settings → Appearance → “Sounds”
Look by time of day

One look in the morning, another in the evening, a third at night. Changes by itself.

📍 Where: Settings → Appearance → “Look by time of day”
Dock
Pinned apps

Drag an icon to change the order; right-click to remove or pin.

📍 Where: Settings → Dock → “Pinned programs”
Second dock

Its own set of programs at another edge: games on the left, work at the bottom.

📍 Where: Settings → Dock → “Second dock”, or right-click an icon → “To the second dock”
Icon groups

Several programs behind one icon — like a folder on a phone.

📍 Where: Right-click an icon → “To group”
Window previews

Hover over an open program's icon — its windows appear. Clicking an icon with several windows lets you pick one.

📍 Where: Works by itself
Clock, sound and language in the dock

On the right of the dock — like the taskbar: language, volume, battery, clock. The mouse wheel changes the volume.

📍 Where: Settings → “Right side of the dock”
Island
The Island at the top of the screen

A living pill: time, weather, music, notifications. Hover — it opens.

📍 Where: Settings → “Island”
Where and what the Island looks like

Left, center or right, shape, your own background and outline color. Faster: Alt + drag the Island.

📍 Where: Settings → “Island” → look
Music and lyrics

Cover, seek, volume and song lyrics in time right on the pill.

📍 Where: Island → “Music”
Karaoke

Song lyrics full screen over blurred wallpaper. Esc — close.

📍 Where: Island → “Music” → “Karaoke”
ChatGPT and other AI chats

A chat right in the Island: sign in to your account once — and it's always at hand.

📍 Where: Island → “AI”
Files from your phone

Point your phone camera at the QR code — photos, files and text fly over Wi-Fi, no cables or cloud.

📍 Where: Island → “Shelf” → QR code
Drop a file on the Island

Drop a file on the pill: zip it, convert a picture to another format or put it on the shelf.

📍 Where: Drag a file onto the Island
Reminders in plain words

Type “tomorrow at 15:00 call mom” — the Island will remind you and show a countdown 15 minutes before.

📍 Where: Island → “Today”
Modes: Game, Night, Work

In games the Island doesn't distract, at night the screen gets warmer, at work chats stay quiet. They turn on by themselves.

📍 Where: Settings → System → “Modes”
Desktop
Live wallpaper

Video, GIF, live scenes and the “Dock wallpapers” set. Your own file works too.

📍 Where: Tray → “Live wallpapers…”
Depth with the mouse

The wallpaper shifts slightly after the cursor — the desktop feels 3D.

📍 Where: Settings → Desktop → “Depth with the mouse”
Wallpaper to music and weather

The wallpaper glows to the music; if it rains or snows outside, it does on the wallpaper too.

📍 Where: Settings → Desktop
Widgets

34 widgets: clock, weather, vinyl, calculator, moon, timer, disks… Any size, frosted glass, ready-made sets.

📍 Where: Tray → “Widgets…”
Stickers and labels

Any picture, GIF or your text in any font right on the desktop.

📍 Where: “Edit desktop” → “Sticker” / “Label”
New Year mood

The garland on the dock blinks to the music, snow on the wallpaper, a New Year countdown in the Island.

📍 Where: Settings → Desktop → “New Year mood”
Hot corners

Mouse to a screen corner — desktop, search, all windows or a screenshot.

📍 Where: Settings → Search and windows → “Set up corners…”
For videos and friends
Looks show

Dock flips through looks with big titles — start screen recording and your video is ready.

📍 Where: Looks → “Looks show”
Desktop picture

A beautiful framed picture of your desktop — to post or send.

📍 Where: Looks → “Desktop snapshot”
Share look

A code for your look: a friend pastes it — and gets the same desktop.

📍 Where: Looks → “Share look” / “Paste code…”
Community looks

The best looks from other users — turned on in one click. You can send yours to the gallery via the bot.

📍 Where: Looks → “Community looks”
Useful
Search your computer

Programs, files, calculator, exchange rates (“100 usd”). Default — Alt + Space.

📍 Where: Alt + Space
Screenshot with annotations

Select part of the screen, add an arrow, frame or text. Default — Ctrl + Alt + S.

📍 Where: Ctrl + Alt + S
Clipboard history

Everything you copied recently. Default — Ctrl + Alt + V.

📍 Where: Ctrl + Alt + V
All windows as tiles

All open windows at once — pick the one you need. Default — Ctrl + Alt + ↑ or a screen corner.

📍 Where: Ctrl + Alt + ↑
Side panel

Mouse to the screen edge — search, quick buttons, music and all apps.

📍 Where: Mouse to the screen edge or Ctrl + Alt + A
Focus

25 minutes without distractions, then a break. Weekly stats — in a widget.

📍 Where: Control center → “Focus”
Warm screen

Less blue light in the evening — easier on the eyes.

📍 Where: Control center → “Warm screen”
Translate from the screen

“Text from screen” → the “Translate” button: the translation goes straight to the clipboard.

📍 Where: Island → “Text from screen”
Settings backup

All settings and your looks — in one file, to move to another computer.

📍 Where: Settings → System → “Backup”
Report a problem

One button — and the author gets a description and a self-check. The reply comes in Telegram.

📍 Where: Tray → “Report a problem…”
